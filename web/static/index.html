Correct the names of your sequences

A name is "gene*allele", for example IGHV1-69*01, and the gene must be in the gene list. Names you correct here are used for this run; your file is not changed. The list stays in the order of your file while you edit, and each line shows at once whether its name now leads to a gene.

Gene list:

The gene list says where each gene lies on the reference genome. An allele is matched to a gene by the part of its name before *. Add a gene, remove one, or load your own list (tab-separated: gene, build, contig, start, end, strand). Your list is kept in this browser.
